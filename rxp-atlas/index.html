Levijath & Pals 60 Speedleveling

The Levijath RestedXP v2 route from 1 to 60. Open a zone section to see where you walk on each map, with numbered stops that match the quest steps below it.

Accept pick upDo objectiveTurn in hand inSM dungeon stepMap: line = your path, numbered dot = quest stop, green dot = section start. Hover a step to find it on the map.

Maps are drawn from the guide's own waypoints on a 0–100 zone grid (the same coordinates as the in-game map and Wowhead), zoomed to the part of each zone you visit, so without map pictures they show the shape and order of the route rather than terrain. On the website, zone pictures placed in a maps/ folder are drawn underneath automatically. Hearthstone, flight and boat jumps break the line. Built for normal (1x) XP with Uldaman and BRD switched off. Quest names link to Wowhead (Forever).
